# Plumas Working Forests

**A data-backed case for industrial forestry that thins, re-enters, and regenerates without clearcut plantations and broadcast herbicide.**

Published by the **Plumas Grassroots Collective** alongside [spraymapca.org](https://www.spraymapca.org/). Live site tab: **Working Forests**.

---

## Stance (read this first)

1. **Logging stays.** Mills, loggers, truckers, and private timberland are part of this landscape and of any serious solution.
2. **Implementation is industry’s call.** It is their timber, their business, and their land (or their federal contract). This program does not mandate practices. It lays out a silvicultural and economic case, with numbers and tests, so owners and mills can adopt, adapt, or reject it.
3. **Data decides.** If volume, cost, or mill-sort evidence **does not** support matching current harvest levels under this system, we say so. Optimism is not a substitute for MBF, stumpage, and mill offtake.
4. **spraymapca’s role** is transparency on chemical use (Map / Data / Science) plus a clear alternative description (Working Forests). Pressure without a workable substitute is noise; a substitute without volume math is wishful.

## One-page pitch

| Status quo | Working-forest case |
|---|---|
| Clearcut large units → plant → free-to-grow with **glyphosate / hexazinone / imazapyr** | Variable-density thin + small gaps → natural or minimal plant → **mechanical/manual release first** |
| High MBF **per acre at one entry**, long wait, chemical dependency | Lower MBF per entry; **more acres and more entries** can match landscape MBF **if** the data say so |
| Forest look resets for decades; watershed chemical load | Continuous canopy character; chemicals not required for the model |

**Tagline:** Harvest the wood. Keep the forest. Skip the spray — *if the numbers work.*

## What “success” means for this repo work

- A forester or mill manager can read the Standard and Economics and know **exactly** what is claimed and what is still unproven.
- The site tab does not overclaim; it points at open questions.
- No claim that industry “must” convert. No claim of 501(c)(3) tax status.

## What success does *not* mean yet

- Signed THPs or USFS contracts using this standard.
- Proven Plumas-specific volume parity (needs mill interviews + CalTREES/FACTS harvest stats — see ECONOMICS.md).
