# Policy Asks — Optional Public Levers

**Priority order:** (1) data and voluntary pilots, (2) cost-share that closes a **measured** gap, (3) public-land prescription alternatives.  
**Not on this list:** banning private harvest; seizing management of private timberland.

Private implementation remains the owner’s business. Policy is about **public land**, **public money**, and **transparent rules**.

---

## 1. Data (do first)

| Ask | Who | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Harvest acres and volume by silvicultural method | CAL FIRE CalTREES / open data | Baseline for volume parity |
| USFS cut/sold + activity volumes for Plumas NF | USFS | Public-land baseline |
| Federal pesticide quantity (PUPs / use reports) | USFS R5 FOIA | Already in spraymapca records-request track |
| Non-confidential mill capacity summaries | Mills / industry associations | T2 test |

Without these, policy that claims “volume neutral” is guesswork.

---

## 2. Cost-share (only after cost gap is real)

| Ask | Who | Design |
|---|---|---|
| Mechanical / manual release cost-share vs chemical free-to-grow | CAL FIRE Forest Health, GGRF, NRCS, SNC, others | Pay the **documented increment**, not a blank anti-spray bonus |
| Thinning utilization / biomass transport support | State / federal fuels programs | Only where offtake exists |
| Monitoring grants for voluntary pilots | NFF, SNC, private foundations | Buy proof, not slogans |

**Condition:** Publish the $/ac gap. If mechanical is already cheaper, do not invent subsidy.

---

## 3. Public land (USFS Plumas NF)

| Ask | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| NEPA alternatives that use matrix thin + small gaps + **no broadcast free-to-grow** | Project-level alternatives / mitigation |
| Contract and timber-sale specs that match the Standard’s chemical hierarchy | Prospectus / stewardship agreement language |
| Community Protection and fuels projects: commercial thin as end-state **forest**, not plantation conversion | Purpose & need + design criteria |

These are **requests into public processes**, not private land mandates.

---

## 4. Private land rules (careful)

| Ask | Note |
|---|---|
| Clear disclosure of planned herbicide in THP / public documents | Transparency aligns with spraymapca; does not ban spray |
| Stocking standards that **recognize** non-chemical free-to-grow pathways | Remove accidental bias toward spray if any exists in practice — requires legal/RPF review before claiming |

Do **not** lead with “ban glyphosate on timberland” as the program’s industrial strategy. The Working Forests case is a **substitute system**, not a prohibition campaign. Separate political efforts may exist; they are not required for this repo’s economics test.

---

## 5. Mill and workforce (enabling)

| Ask | Why |
|---|---|
| Public investment in small-log / mass-timber / biomass capacity where markets fail | T2 enabler |
| Training for partial-cut production | T3 enabler |

If offtake and crews do not exist, silviculture on paper fails in the woods.

---

## 6. Draft comment snippet (USFS project)

> We support commercial harvest that meets forest health and volume objectives. We request analysis of an alternative consistent with the Plumas Working Forest Standard: variable-density matrix thinning, group openings generally ≤ 2 acres, retained skips, natural or planted regen as needed, and competition control without broadcast aerial or soil-residual free-to-grow herbicide programs. We ask that the FEIS/EA compare decade-scale volume and cost to the plantation pathway using local mill assumptions. If volume cannot be matched, the document should say so explicitly.

---

## 7. Sequencing reminder

```text
Data → Voluntary pilot actuals → Pass/fail volume & cost
        → then cost-share / NEPA language that depends on those facts
```

Skipping to regulation without T1–T4 evidence weakens the industry case and the community case alike.
