# Stakeholder Map — Offer, Not Demand

**Engagement principle:** We present data and a voluntary standard. Private owners implement only if it works for their business. Public agencies decide under their authorities. Corrections to our numbers are welcome.

---

## 1. Who must be whole if the case is real

| Stakeholder | What “whole” means | What we offer | What we do not do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Industrial private timber** (e.g. large PUR permittees on spraymapca) | NPV, cash flow, regulatory path, mill contracts | Economics test, pilot design, chemical transparency context | Seize management; smear for existing legal ops |
| **Other private / family timber** | Same at smaller scale; often more flexibility | Same Standard; possible pilot fit | Assume they can absorb higher costs without numbers |
| **Contract loggers** | Days of work, safe partial-cut practice, fair rates | Case for **more acres** of commercial thin | Promise jobs we cannot fund |
| **Mills & biomass** | Steady feed, right sorts | Volume worksheet; offtake discussion invites | Claim mill capacity we have not verified |
| **USFS Plumas NF** | Timber + fuels + NEPA defensibility | Prescription language, no-broadcast alternative | Pretend we write their ROD |
| **CAL FIRE / Board of Forestry** | Rules compliance, fire goals | Non-chemical release pathways consistent with stocking | Rewrite FPRs unilaterally |
| **County / Ag Commissioner** | Legal pesticide use reporting | PUR transparency (existing) | Block lawful permits by website |
| **Tribes** | Sovereignty, cultural resources, co-stewardship | Early listen; data sharing | Speak for tribal governments |
| **Community / wells / recreation** | Less broadcast chemistry, forest character, fire | Map + Working Forests case | Promise zero harvest |
| **Plumas Grassroots Collective** | Credibility | Honest pass/fail on volume | Fake success stories |

---

## 2. Engagement sequence (suggested)

1. **Internal:** Publish Standard + Economics + open data gaps (this repo / site tab).  
2. **Data partners:** CalTREES harvest stats; mill public reports; USFS cut/sold.  
3. **Quiet technical conversations** with foresters/RPFs and mill procurement — *request data, not conversion.*  
4. **Voluntary pilot** only if an owner or agency invites.  
5. **Publish pilot metrics** (pass or fail).  
6. **Policy cost-share** only after a measured cost gap (POLICY_ASKS.md).

Do **not** start with ultimatums to private landowners. The map already shows chemical use; the Working Forests tab shows a substitute. Adults can decide.

## 3. Draft outreach tones

### To a private timberland manager (email draft — do not send without approval)

> Subject: Plumas Working Forests — volume test, not a campaign demand  
>  
> We run spraymapca.org (pesticide use transparency for Plumas / CA). We published a voluntary silvicultural case — multi-entry thin + small gaps, no broadcast free-to-grow chemistry — and an explicit **volume parity test**. Implementation is your business if the numbers work; if they do not, we will say so.  
>  
> If you are willing to share non-confidential harvest or cost ranges (or correct our assumptions), we will update the public worksheet. Pilot interest is optional.  
>  
> spraymapca@gmail.com

### To a mill procurement lead

> Subject: Sort mix and capacity — Working Forests volume sketch  
>  
> We are testing whether multi-entry thinning can match decade-scale MBF without clearcut plantations. That fails if mills cannot take the sorts. We are asking for capacity and preferred diameter mix guidance — not a commitment. Happy to share the draft worksheet.

### To USFS planner

> Subject: Alternative prescription language — no broadcast free-to-grow  
>  
> For projects that already propose commercial thin or group selection, we offer a written Standard (matrix/gaps/skips + chemical hierarchy) communities can support and industry can price. We are not asking for zero harvest.

---

## 4. Conflict protocol

- Disputes over **facts** → correct the repo and site; thank the source.  
- Disputes over **values** (how much risk to wells vs how much cash pulse) → state both; do not pretend science erases tradeoffs.  
- Bad-faith attacks either direction → stay on data; no pile-on.
