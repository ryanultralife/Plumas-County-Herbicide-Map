# Sources & Data Gaps

Evidence for **chemical use** on spraymapca is strong (CA PUR + partial USFS FACTS). Evidence for **Plumas-specific volume parity** of Working Forests is **not yet filled** — see ECONOMICS.md. This list separates what we cite from what we still need.

---

## 1. Local chemical & project context

- SprayMap California — map, Data & Trends, Herbicides & Science: https://www.spraymapca.org/  
- California Department of Pesticide Regulation — Pesticide Use Reporting / CalPIP.  
- USFS FACTS activity data (treatment locations/acres; chemical quantities often incomplete without FOIA).  
- Plumas NF Community Protection Project and related NEPA materials (public project pages / SOPA).  
- Repo: `Plumas_Pesticide_Data_Brief_and_Requests.md`, `records-requests/`.

---

## 2. Silviculture (general, peer/agency)

- Franklin, Johnson, and colleagues — ecological forestry / variable-density thinning concepts (legacy, matrix, gaps).  
- USFS density management and Sierra Nevada forest ecology syntheses (PSW GTRs; use current editions when specifying BA/SDI).  
- Continuous cover / selection systems literature (Europe and PNW) as **analogues**, not copy-paste prescriptions for Sierra mixed conifer.  
- UCANR / cooperative extension forestry guidance for California landowners (regen, stocking, fire).

*When writing unit-level BA targets, cite the specific density diagram or forest plan direction used — do not treat this program’s narrative ranges as peer-reviewed site tables.*

---

## 3. Restoration wood supply & pace/scale (California)

- Tahoe–Central Sierra Initiative and related wood supply / restoration assessments (e.g. Mason, Bruce & Girard work for TNC / partners) — **need for utilization** of thin material.  
- Sierra Nevada Conservancy / Watershed Improvement Program materials — pace and scale of forest health work.  
- CAL FIRE Forest Health / GGRF program descriptions — public funding that can close fire-related cost gaps.  
- Market capacity assessments for restoration byproducts (regional CAL FIRE / contractor studies).

These support the claim that **more thinning acres are needed than funding alone can buy** — the commercial bridge argument — not automatic volume parity on every ownership.

---

## 4. Industry structure (Plumas / Sierra)

- Public reporting and news on major private timberland managers and mills in the Northern Sierra (capacity changes over time).  
- USFS cut and sold reports for Region 5 / Plumas NF.  
- CalTREES Timber Harvest Plan data (harvest method fields where available).

**Gap:** In-repo, we have not yet compiled a validated Plumas MBF baseline by method. Until that exists, volume examples remain illustrative.

---

## 5. What would upgrade claims

| Claim level | Required |
|---|---|
| “Herbicides are used heavily in this pathway” | **Met** via PUR / site |
| “An alternative silvicultural system exists on paper” | **Met** via PRESCRIPTION_STANDARD |
| “It matches current harvest levels in Plumas” | **Not met** — needs CalTREES/FACTS + mill + logging cost fills |
| “Owners will adopt it” | **Not a science claim** — market and preference |

---

## 6. Correction policy

Email **spraymapca@gmail.com** with source links or datasets. Material errors in program docs will be corrected in-repo; the site tab will follow. Industry data shared under confidentiality can improve internal worksheets without full public release — but **public volume-parity claims** need publishable inputs or they stay labeled unproven.
