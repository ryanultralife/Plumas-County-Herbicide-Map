## The finding (surfaced on the site)

The Dixie Fire / Greenville watershed **is** monitored — for **post-fire recovery
indicators**: turbidity/sediment, nutrients, bacteria, major ions, and a full
suite of **metals**. But **not one herbicide** applied in these same watersheds
(hexazinone, imazapyr, glyphosate, aminopyralid, …) appears in the sampling
panel. The Forest Service formally *notifies* the Board of its herbicide
operations and the work is tracked for erosion-control (BMP) compliance, yet the
**water itself is never screened for the compounds being sprayed**.

This is the project's central "what isn't tested" gap, now backed by the Board's
own records. It is surfaced in the Science tab card **"Is the water tested for
these herbicides? Not yet."** The structured record is `data/water_monitoring.json`.

Other Water Board units (WDR, etc.) sent additional responses still in the inbox;
those are largely permit/compliance correspondence, not new water-chemistry data.
