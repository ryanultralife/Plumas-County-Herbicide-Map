To the BLM California State Office FOIA Coordinator:

Under the Freedom of Information Act (5 U.S.C. 552), I request copies of the following records held by the Bureau of Land Management for public lands in Plumas, Lassen and Sierra counties, California (Eagle Lake and Mother Lode field offices and any others with jurisdiction), January 1, 2020 to the present:
1. All Pesticide Use Proposals (PUPs) approved, submitted or implemented, including product name and EPA registration number, active ingredient, application rate and amount, treatment location and acres, and method.
2. Pesticide Application Records (PARs) and any annual pesticide-use summaries for these lands.
3. Any environmental documents (EA / CX / DNA) authorizing herbicide use on these lands, and any water-quality or post-treatment monitoring associated with them.
