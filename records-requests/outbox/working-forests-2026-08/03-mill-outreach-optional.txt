I work with Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public map of reported pesticide use in California with a focus on Plumas County and the Northern Sierra. We also publish an open “Working Forests” cost ledger that compares commercial thinning pathways to clearcut / plant / free-to-grow pathways, including with and without broadcast herbicide.

Important: this is not a demand to change your silviculture. Implementation of harvest systems is your business decision on your timber and land. Our goal is accurate public numbers so communities and industry can see the same cost structure.

If those proxies misstate local pond values, we would rather correct them.
