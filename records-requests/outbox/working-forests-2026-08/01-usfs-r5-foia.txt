To the Region 5 FOIA Officer,

Under the Freedom of Information Act, 5 U.S.C. § 552, I request electronic copies of the following records concerning the Plumas National Forest (all ranger districts) for the period January 1, 2020 to the present:

1. All Pesticide-Use Proposals (Form FS-2100-2 / "PUP") approved, submitted, or implemented for the Plumas National Forest, including active ingredient(s), product name/EPA registration number, target pest, treatment acreage, application method, total quantity applied, and treatment location.

2. Any annual or periodic pesticide/herbicide use reports or summaries compiled at the Forest or Region 5 level that include Plumas National Forest applications (e.g., active-ingredient totals and acres treated by year).

3. Contract line-item costs, bid abstracts, award documents, or invoice summaries for reforestation and vegetation-management services on the Plumas National Forest, including unit prices ($/acre or $/tree) where held for:
   - planting / seedling establishment
   - site preparation
   - chemical release / free-to-grow herbicide treatments
   - manual or mechanical release / brushing
   - mastication or related vegetation control

4. Timber sale appraisal worksheets, R5 LogCost exports, R5 HaulCost exports, or equivalent records for Plumas National Forest timber sales sufficient to show stump-to-truck logging cost and haul cost assumptions used in advertised rates (by species/product where available).

5. Stewardship IRTC/IRSC or similar project cost tables that separately list service work (thin, pile, burn, plant, spray) versus timber value, for projects on or primarily affecting the Plumas National Forest.

6. Any treatment maps, GIS files, or shapefiles of treatment units associated with items 1–3 above.

Please provide records in electronic format (searchable PDF, spreadsheet, native file, or shapefile/GeoJSON) where available.

I request a fee waiver because disclosure is in the public interest and not for commercial use. The records will be used to publish transparent, noncommercial cost and chemical-use information for rural communities on spraymapca.org (Plumas Grassroots Collective). If the fee-waiver request is denied, please notify me before incurring costs exceeding $25.
