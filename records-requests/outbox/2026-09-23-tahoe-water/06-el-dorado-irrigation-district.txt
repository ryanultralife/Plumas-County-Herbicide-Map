Dear El Dorado Irrigation District records staff,

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public, noncommercial map of California's reported pesticide use and of the water testing done near it. We have mapped the state's herbicide-use records for El Dorado County and are compiling the water monitoring for the same watersheds so residents can see both in one place.

Your treated-water compliance results are public through the State Water Board, and we are pulling those directly, so this request does not ask for them. Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide, for January 1, 2018 to the present:
   1. Raw-water or source-water monitoring results (Jenkinson Lake, Folsom Lake, canal and ditch intakes) for any herbicide or other pesticide analyte (for example hexazinone, imazapyr, glyphosate, triclopyr, aminopyralid, 2,4-D), with the sampling location for each;
   2. The sections of your most recent watershed sanitary survey or source-water assessment that address pesticide or herbicide use in your watersheds;
   3. Any notices you received of herbicide applications on land draining to your intakes or canals, and any correspondence about testing for them.
If no herbicide analyte has been run, a short statement saying so fully answers item 1. CSV or Excel files, or links to posted reports, are ideal.

We will credit on the site any supplier that tests for these herbicides.

This request serves the public interest in transparency and is not for commercial use. Please advise of any direct-cost fees before they exceed $25, and let us know the expected response date.

Thank you,
