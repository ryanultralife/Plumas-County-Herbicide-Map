Dear Lahontan Water Board records staff,

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public, noncommercial map of California's reported pesticide use and of the water testing done near it. We have mapped state pesticide-use records across Placer, Nevada, El Dorado and Alpine counties and are now compiling the water-quality monitoring for the Lake Tahoe basin and the east-slope rivers so residents can see both in one place.

1) Records request - what the Board already holds.
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide copies of the following for the Lake Tahoe basin and the Truckee, Carson and Walker river watersheds within California, January 1, 2018 to the present:
   a. Any surface-water, lake, or groundwater monitoring results the Board holds or oversees that include a herbicide or other pesticide analyte (for example glyphosate, triclopyr, endothall, hexazinone, imazapyr, 2,4-D, aminopyralid), with station identifiers and locations;
   b. Monitoring plans, results and reports required under any permit or authorization for aquatic herbicide use in the basin, including the Tahoe Keys lagoons control methods test;
   c. Notices of intent, application logs or annual reports the Board received for herbicide applications under the statewide aquatic weed control or vector control general permits in these watersheds;
   d. A list of the Board's ambient or SWAMP monitoring stations in these watersheds and the analytes each is run for.
Electronic, machine-readable formats (CSV or Excel), or links to where the results are already posted (CEDEN, GeoTracker, the Board's website), are ideal and would fully satisfy an item.

2) A forward-looking question.
Where routine monitoring in these watersheds does not include the herbicides applied on nearby forest and roadside land, we would encourage adding them, and are glad to share the application records we have mapped.

This request serves the public interest in transparency and is not for commercial use. Please advise of any direct-cost fees before they exceed $25, and let us know the expected response date.

Thank you,
