Dear Ms. Kasich and the Central Valley Water Board (Region 5) team,

Thank you again for the Dixie Fire and Greenville watershed records you provided for Plumas County this summer; they are now on spraymapca.org. We are extending the same work south to Placer, Nevada, El Dorado and Alpine counties, where we have mapped the state's reported herbicide use.

1) Records request.
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide, for the portions of Placer, Nevada, El Dorado and Alpine counties in Region 5, January 1, 2018 to the present:
   a. Any surface-water or receiving-water monitoring results that include a herbicide or other pesticide analyte (for example hexazinone, imazapyr, glyphosate, triclopyr, aminopyralid, 2,4-D), with station identifiers and locations;
   b. Herbicide-use notices and monitoring or reporting submitted under the Board's timber-harvest, post-fire recovery or forest-management orders in these counties (for example Caldor Fire and Mosquito Fire recovery work);
   c. A list of the Board's ambient or SWAMP monitoring stations in these counties and the analytes each is run for.
If the answer for an item is that no herbicide analyte has been run, a short statement saying so fully answers it. Links to CEDEN or GeoTracker, or CSV/Excel files, are ideal.

2) A forward-looking question.
As with Plumas, we would encourage adding the forestry herbicides applied in these watersheds to post-fire and ambient monitoring, and can share the application records we have mapped.

This request serves the public interest in transparency and is not for commercial use. Please advise of any direct-cost fees before they exceed $25, and let us know the expected response date.

Thank you,
