Dear Tahoe Water Suppliers Association,

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public, noncommercial map of California's reported pesticide use and of the water testing done near it. We are compiling the water-quality monitoring for the Lake Tahoe basin so residents can see it next to the herbicide-use records we have mapped for Placer and El Dorado counties.

Your members' treated-water compliance results are public through the State Water Board, and we are pulling those directly. What the state files do not show is the raw lake-water and source-water monitoring your members and the Association carry out. We would be grateful for:
   1. Raw or source-water monitoring results from Lake Tahoe intakes, January 1, 2018 to the present, for any herbicide or other pesticide analyte (for example triclopyr, endothall, glyphosate, 2,4-D), with the intake or station location for each;
   2. Any monitoring your members did at their intakes in connection with the Tahoe Keys lagoons aquatic herbicide test, and the results;
   3. The Association's source-water protection or watershed sanitary survey sections that address pesticide or herbicide use in the basin.
Links to posted reports, or CSV/Excel files, are ideal. The Association is a private body, so this is a request rather than a Public Records Act demand; if a member agency is the right custodian, please point us to it and we will ask them directly.

We will credit any supplier that tests for these herbicides on the site.

Thank you,
