Dear Ms. Kasich and the Central Valley Water Board (Region 5) team,

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public, noncommercial map of pesticide use and water data for Plumas County and the Northern Sierra. We are compiling the water-quality monitoring that happens across the county so residents can see it in one place.

1) Records request - what you already monitor.
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide copies of all surface-water monitoring the Board holds or oversees in Plumas County for January 1, 2018 to the present, including:
   - WDR / receiving-water monitoring for each permitted discharger (e.g. the community wastewater plants);
   - any ambient or SWAMP monitoring sites in the county;
   - the Dixie Fire recovery monitoring (the DF-1/2/3 stations and any others);
   with station identifiers and locations (latitude/longitude or a map) for each.
Electronic, machine-readable formats (CSV or Excel), or a link to where the results are posted, are ideal.

2) A forward-looking question - herbicide testing.
As you know from the Nonpoint Source Unit's records, this monitoring does not currently include the forestry herbicides being applied across these same watersheds (hexazinone, imazapyr, glyphosate, aminopyralid and others), several of which are water-mobile and move toward community water sources. We would encourage the Board to consider adding these analytes to Dixie Fire / timber-order and ambient monitoring, and are glad to help document the need.

This serves the public interest in transparency and is not for commercial use. Please advise of any direct-cost fees before they exceed $25, and let us know the expected response date.
