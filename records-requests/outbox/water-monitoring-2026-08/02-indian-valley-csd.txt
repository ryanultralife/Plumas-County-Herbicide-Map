Dear Indian Valley Community Services District team,

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public, noncommercial map of pesticide use and water data for Plumas County and the Northern Sierra. We are compiling the water-quality monitoring that happens across the county so residents can see it in one place.

1) Records request - what you already monitor.
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide copies of routine water-quality monitoring records for January 1, 2018 to the present, including:
   - source-water and finished drinking-water sampling results (and any raw-water, spring, or well results);
   - any receiving-water or wastewater-effluent monitoring the district conducts;
   - the list of your sampling sites/stations with their locations (street address, latitude/longitude, or a map).
Electronic, machine-readable formats (CSV or Excel), or a link to where the results are posted, are ideal.

2) A forward-looking question - herbicide testing.
We have confirmed, from the Central Valley Water Board's own records, that monitoring in these watersheds does not currently test for the forestry herbicides being applied upstream - hexazinone, imazapyr, glyphosate, aminopyralid and others, several of which are water-mobile and go onto watersheds above community water sources. We are not asking you to change any operations; we would simply encourage adding these analytes to future sampling where feasible, and we will gladly feature any system that does.

This serves the public interest in transparency and is not for commercial use. Please advise of any direct-cost fees before they exceed $25, and let us know the expected response date.
