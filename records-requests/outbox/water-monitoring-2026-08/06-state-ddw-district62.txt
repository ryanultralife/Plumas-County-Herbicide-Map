Dear Division of Drinking Water (County Engagement Unit / District 62),

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public, noncommercial map of pesticide use and water data for Plumas County and the Northern Sierra. We are compiling the water-quality monitoring that happens across the county so residents can see it in one place.

1) Records request - what you already monitor.
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide copies of the compliance and source-water monitoring data your Division holds for all public water systems in Plumas County for January 1, 2018 to the present - or a pointer to the corresponding SDWIS / Drinking Water Watch export - together with the list of systems and their source and sampling-point locations.
Electronic, machine-readable formats (CSV or Excel), or a link to where the results are posted, are ideal.

2) A forward-looking question - herbicide testing.
None of this monitoring currently screens for the forestry herbicides applied across these watersheds (hexazinone, imazapyr, glyphosate, aminopyralid and others), several of which are water-mobile. We would encourage the Division to consider whether source-water assessments and monitoring for affected systems should include these analytes.

This serves the public interest in transparency and is not for commercial use. Please advise of any direct-cost fees before they exceed $25, and let us know the expected response date.
