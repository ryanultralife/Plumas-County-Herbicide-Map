Dear Plumas County Environmental Health team,

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a public, noncommercial map of pesticide use and water data for Plumas County and the Northern Sierra. We are compiling the water-quality monitoring that happens across the county so residents can see it in one place.

1) Records request - what you already monitor.
Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide copies of the water-quality monitoring records your office holds for the small water systems and private wells it oversees in Plumas County for January 1, 2018 to the present, plus the locations of those systems/sampling points where public.
Electronic, machine-readable formats (CSV or Excel), or a link to where the results are posted, are ideal.

2) A forward-looking question - herbicide testing.
None of this monitoring currently tests for the forestry herbicides applied across these watersheds (hexazinone, imazapyr, glyphosate, aminopyralid and others), several of which are water-mobile and reach the wells and springs many residents rely on. We would encourage adding these analytes where feasible.

This serves the public interest in transparency and is not for commercial use. Please advise of any direct-cost fees before they exceed $25, and let us know the expected response date.
