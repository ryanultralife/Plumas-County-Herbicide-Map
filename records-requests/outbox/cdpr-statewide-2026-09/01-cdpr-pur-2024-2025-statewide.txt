SUBJECT: Public Records Act request - Pesticide Use Report data, calendar years 2024 and 2025 (and 2026 to date), all 58 counties, in the Department's annual-archive record layout
DRAFTED: 2026-09-23 (unsent; portal submission)

To the Public Records Coordinator, California Department of Pesticide Regulation:

I work with the Plumas Grassroots Collective on spraymapca.org, a free, noncommercial public map
built from the Department's Pesticide Use Report (PUR) data. The map now carries the Department's
published PUR for every county from 2020 through 2023. The Department has not yet published 2024,
and 2025 will follow it by about a year, so the most recent three years of pesticide use in
California are invisible to the public outside the handful of counties that release their own
records.

Under the California Public Records Act (Gov. Code section 7920.000 et seq.), please provide copies
of the following records as they exist in the Department's PUR database on the date of your
response:

1. Pesticide Use Report data for calendar year 2024, all 58 counties.
2. Pesticide Use Report data for calendar year 2025, all 58 counties.
3. Pesticide Use Report data for January 1, 2026 to the date of your response, all 58 counties, to
   the extent it has been entered.

Preferred record layout: the same layout as the Department's published annual PUR archives
(the per-county comprehensive-use text files, "udcYY_CC.txt", with the standard fields: use_no,
prodno, product_name, chem_code, chemname, prodchem_pct, lbs_chm_used, lbs_prd_used, amt_prd_used,
unit_of_meas, acre_planted, unit_planted, acre_treated, unit_treated, applic_cnt, applic_dt,
applic_time, county_cd, county, base_ln_mer, township, tship_dir, range, range_dir, section,
site_loc_id, grower_id, license_no, planting_seq, aer_gnd_ind, site_code, site_name, qualify_cd,
batch_no, document_no, summary_cd, record_id, comtrs, error_flag), together with that year's
product, chemical, site and county lookup tables. If that is not practical, the 37-column extract
layout the Department produced for request #26-637 ("vukich_spraymap_26-637.xlsx") is equally
useful, extended to all counties.

Precedent, and why this is a records request rather than a data-products request: on request
#26-637 the Department released 2020 through 2024 PUR data for five Northern Sierra counties on
July 8, 2026, before the 2024 year was published. That extract has been on the public map since,
labelled as preliminary. We are asking for the same records for the remaining counties, on the same
terms.

Preliminary data: we understand that 2024 has not completed the Department's publication review
and that 2025 and 2026 are still being entered. We ask for the data as it currently exists,
together with (a) the extraction date and (b) any note the Department can provide on counties,
months, or record types known to be incomplete, so the map can label the data honestly. We accept
the data with any such caveats. If publication of the 2024 archive is imminent, please tell us the
expected posting date and we will withdraw item 1.

Format and delivery: machine-readable (tab- or comma-delimited text, or Excel) in a zip archive.
A NextRequest upload, a download link, or an SFTP location are all fine; file sizes on the order of
the Department's own annual archives (a few hundred megabytes per year) present no difficulty.

Confidentiality: these are the same records the Department publishes annually. We are not
requesting personal information beyond the coded permit and site identifiers the archives already
contain.

Fees: this request serves the public interest in government transparency and is noncommercial;
please waive fees if possible. If costs would exceed $25, please contact me first with an estimate.

Under Gov. Code section 7922.535, please let me know within 10 days whether responsive records
exist and when they will be produced. If any portion is withheld, please cite the specific
exemption.

Thank you,
